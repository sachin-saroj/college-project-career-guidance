# User Manual

Welcome to CareerSathi! This guide will help you navigate the platform and make the most out of our career guidance tools.

## 1. Registration and Login

- **Registration**: Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. Enter your Name, Email, Password, and select your role ("Student").
- **Login**: If you already have an account, click "Login". Enter your email and password.
- **Forgot Password**: Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a secure reset link via email.

## 2. Dashboard

Upon logging in, you will be taken to your personalized Dashboard. Here you can see:
- Quick stats (Assessments Taken, Saved Careers).
- Pending tasks.
- Quick links to the Assessment Engine and Mentor Chat.

## 3. Taking a Career Assessment

1. Navigate to the **Assessments** tab from the sidebar.
2. Click **Start New Assessment**.
3. Answer the series of questions. There are no right or wrong answers—be honest! The system evaluates Aptitude, Interest, and Personality.
4. Submit the assessment to instantly view your results and top career matches.

## 4. Viewing Recommendations

Based on your assessment scores, the **Recommendations** tab will display careers that match your profile.
- Each career card displays a match percentage (Confidence Score).
- Click on a career to view required skills, estimated salary ranges, and recommended learning resources.

## 5. Chatting with the AI Mentor

Have a question about a career or need advice on what course to take?
1. Open the **AI Mentor** tab.
2. Type your question in the chat box (e.g., "What is the difference between a Data Scientist and a Data Analyst?").
3. Our Gemini-powered AI will respond instantly with tailored advice.

## 6. Resources & Bookmarking

Explore the **Resources** library to find curated courses, scholarships, and articles.
- Use the search bar or category filters to find what you need.
- Click the **Bookmark** icon to save a resource for later.
- View your saved items in the **Bookmarks** tab.

## 7. Resume & Portfolio Builder

1. Navigate to the **Resume Builder**.
2. Fill out your Personal Info, Education, and Experience.
3. Choose a template and click **Export PDF** to download your professional resume.
4. Go to the **Portfolio** tab to generate a public link to showcase your projects and skills to potential employers.
